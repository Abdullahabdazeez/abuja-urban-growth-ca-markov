## First public release

This release presents the completed quantity-constrained CA–Markov urban growth scenario for Abuja from 2025 to 2035.

### Included

- Derived 2025 baseline LULC and predicted 2035 LULC
- Built-up expansion and transition rasters
- Browser-uploadable urban-expansion suitability surface
- 2015–2025 Markov transition probabilities
- Land-cover area and net-change statistics
- Historical validation summary
- Publication-ready maps and charts
- Python reproduction and repository-validation scripts
- Methodology, results and limitations documentation

### Principal finding

Built-up land is projected to increase by **183.45 km² (15.71%)**, from **1,167.85 km² in 2025** to **1,351.30 km² in 2035**. Cropland records the largest predicted decline at **153.74 km²**.

### Interpretation note

Historical validation produced **49.27% overall accuracy** and **0.366 Kappa**. The 2035 map should therefore be used as a strategic planning scenario rather than a deterministic forecast.
