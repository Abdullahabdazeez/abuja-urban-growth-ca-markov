# Abuja CA–Markov Urban Growth Prediction — Technical Summary

The project used historical LULC and a quantity-constrained CA–Markov allocation to simulate Abuja's 2035 urban pattern. Built-up area increases from 1,167.85 km² to 1,351.30 km², a gain of 183.45 km² (15.71%). Cropland provides most of the projected conversion area. Allocation matched 98.96% of the Markov-derived built-up demand and prioritised cells with high urban suitability.

Historical validation produced 49.27% overall accuracy and 0.366 Kappa. The 2035 result is therefore presented as a strategic scenario for growth management and field verification rather than a deterministic forecast.
