# Results

Built-up land is estimated at **1,167.85 km²** in 2025 and **1,351.30 km²** in the 2035 scenario. This represents a net increase of **183.45 km²**, equivalent to **15.71%** relative to the 2025 built-up area.

The model required **206,027** new built-up pixels and allocated **203,889**, leaving a difference of **2,138 pixels**.

## Validation

The historical simulation produced **49.27% Overall Accuracy** and **Kappa 0.366** on **1,500 validation samples**.

The validation result means the scenario should not be read as a certain forecast. Its strongest use is broad planning screening and discussion about where urban growth pressure may emerge and where infrastructure, development control and environmental safeguards may need earlier attention.

## Planning interpretation

The scenario provides a spatial basis for reviewing likely growth corridors against roads, utilities, schools, healthcare, drainage, public transport and environmental constraints. It should be updated and checked against newer imagery, current plans and field evidence before site-specific decisions are made.
