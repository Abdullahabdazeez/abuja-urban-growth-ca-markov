# Results

## Built-up growth

Built-up land increases from **1,167.85 km²** in 2025 to **1,351.30 km²** in 2035. The projected increase is **183.45 km²**, equivalent to **15.71%**.

## Land converted to development

Cropland records the largest predicted decline, falling by **153.74 km²**. Vegetation declines by **11.00 km²**, while bare land declines by **18.70 km²**.

## Suitability allocation

The mean suitability score of allocated expansion cells is **0.819**, compared with **0.624** for other finite candidate cells. This confirms that the allocation preferentially selected higher-ranked locations.

## Validation

Historical validation used **1,500** samples and produced **49.27%** overall accuracy with **0.366** Kappa. These values support cautious interpretation of spatial detail.
