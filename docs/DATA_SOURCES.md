# Data Sources

## Primary analytical inputs
- Landsat-derived land-cover maps used for historical transition analysis.
- Federal Capital Territory boundary shapefile used to define the study area.
- Urban-expansion suitability raster used to rank candidate locations for future built-up allocation.
- 2015-2025 Markov transition-probability tables.
- 2025-2035 LULC area tables.
- CA-Markov prediction and validation summaries.

## Spatial framework
- Study area: Federal Capital Territory, Abuja, Nigeria.
- Raster resolution: 30 m.
- Projection used by the scenario rasters: WGS 84 / UTM Zone 32N (EPSG:32632).
- Model: Quantity-Constrained CA-Markov.

## Scenario interpretation
Historical validation achieved **49.27% Overall Accuracy** and **Kappa 0.366** on **1,500 samples**. The 2035 output is therefore presented as a strategic planning scenario for broad screening and discussion rather than a deterministic or parcel-level forecast.
